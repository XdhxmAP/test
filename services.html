Services

Everything an influencer campaign needs — handled.

Voksera is a full-service partner for streetwear and youth-fashion brands. You bring the product and the goal. We bring the creators, the creative, and the management to make it land.

01 / What we handle

One partner for the whole campaign.

01

Creator sourcing & vetting

We match your brand to male micro-influencers (10k+) whose audience overlaps with yours — and who'd genuinely wear your label. Every creator is vetted for fit, engagement, and content quality before we ever suggest them.

02

Concept & creative direction

We build each collaboration around your drop and your goal, so the content has a reason to exist beyond "here's a product". The idea comes first; the post follows.

03

Scriptwriting

We write the script for every piece — hook, framing, call to action — tailored to each creator's voice and your brand's tone. Native to the feed, never copy-pasted.

04

Campaign management

Briefing, scheduling, approvals, follow-up — we run the entire collaboration end to end, so you're never chasing creators or managing logistics.

05

Reach & reporting

Your content goes live to engaged audiences, and we report back on how it performed. You keep the content to reuse across your own channels.

02 / Who it's for

Built for founder-led streetwear brands.

Voksera is a fit if you run a streetwear or youth-fashion label with its own shop and a brand you care about getting right.

You want more reach and stronger content, but you don't have the time — or the desire — to find creators, negotiate, brief, and chase deliverables yourself. That's exactly the part we take off your plate.

  • Streetwear & youth fashion
  • Own online shop
  • Founder-led
  • Ready to grow reach
03 / How pricing works

Simple, and agreed up front.

You pay the influencer fee, plus a Voksera commission on top. That's it.

The commission covers everything we do — sourcing, creative direction, scripting, and full campaign management. We agree the numbers before anything starts, so there are no surprises and nothing to sign before you've seen what we propose.
